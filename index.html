I’m looking for

Floor

Shows only the containers, safes, loose items, resources and zones of this floor. Extractions, infill points, doors, switches, bosses and traders stay visible. I grouped the floors by height; in the game they may be called something else.

Resources (gatherable)

Count on this map. Under “Layers” you can show or hide each kind.

My infill

Highlights the extraction that the level data says conflicts with this infill point (probably not active then).

Details

Click a zone or a point to see which kinds of loot drop there and where the information comes from.

Layers

For layers with an arrow, the arrow opens the single kinds: the box in front switches the whole layer, the boxes below switch each kind on its own.

Hidden kinds are not drawn. The zone contents (bars in the detail card) still count all containers. Your browser remembers the selection if it allows that.
